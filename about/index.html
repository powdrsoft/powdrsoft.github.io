    About

    Powdrsoft Solutions AB is an independent IT consultancy. We help teams build, ship and run software, and we share what we learn along the way.

    What we do

    • Consulting: software development and DevOps, from the first line of code to systems running in production.
    • Training: passing on hands-on experience to development teams.
    • Products: sales of software products and services in the IT area.

    Tobias Ericsson

    Tobias Ericsson is the founder of Powdrsoft Solutions AB and works as an independent contractor.

    He spent many years as a Java backend developer before broadening into DevOps. Today he works across the whole delivery chain: mobile and frontend, backend services, build pipelines and operations.

    That breadth lets him see the whole system, and remove the friction between writing code and running it.
