    How to force an app to quit on your Mac

    Option + Command + Esc

    Or choose Force Quit from the Apple () menu in the upper-left corner of your screen.

    How to take a screenshot on your Mac

    Shift + Command + 3 -> the whole screen

    Shift + Command + 4 -> a portion of the screen

    Shift + Command + 4 + Space -> a window in the screen

    Function-Delete to forward delete

    Fn + Delete -> deletes the character to right of the cursor

    Control + D -> deletes the character to right of the cursor

    Command + Delete -> deletes whole word

    Option + Delete -> deletes whole word

    Line-level cursor movement

    Control-A: Move to the beginning of the line or paragraph.

    Control-E: Move to the end of a line or paragraph.

    Control-K: Delete the text between the insertion point and the end of the line or paragraph.

    Control-U: Delete the text between the insertion point and the beginning of the line or paragraph.

    Control-P: Move up one line.

    Control-N: Move down one line.

    Character-level cursor movement

    Control-F: Move one character forward, Equivalent to Right Arrow.

    Control-B: Move one character backward, Equivalent to Left Arrow.

    Control-H: Delete the character to the left of the insertion point.

    Control-D: Delete the character to the right of the insertion point.

    Word-level cursor movement

    Option–Left Arrow or Option–Control-F: Move the insertion point to the beginning of the previous word.

    Option–Right Arrow or Option–Control-B: Move the insertion point to the end of the next word.

    Option-Delete: Delete the word to the left of the insertion point.
